⚡ R4GESTORM Community Partnership Guide

Interested in partnering with R4GESTORM Community? We'd be happy to work with your server.

To begin, join our server and contact either the Owner, Advertising Strategist, or Brand Manager. Once we've discussed the details and reached an agreement, we'll exchange advertisements and post them in each other's partnership channels.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌀 Low Storm Partnership

For smaller and growing communities.

Requirements

▸ Minimum 50 members
▸ SFW and non-toxic environment
▸ Dedicated partnership channel

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌪 Giant Storm Partnership

For larger and established communities.

Requirements

▸ Minimum 500 members
▸ SFW and non-toxic environment
▸ Active community
▸ Complies with Discord ToS and Community Guidelines

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📋 General Requirements

▸ All servers must be intended for users aged 13+
▸ No botted or artificially inflated member counts
▸ Partnership advertisements must remain visible in the partnership channel

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠ Important Notes

▸ The person requesting the partnership must remain in R4GESTORM Community. If they leave, their server advertisement may be removed.

▸ Failure to comply with any of the requirements may result in:
• Partnership termination
• Advertisement removal
• Permanent blacklist from future partnerships

▸ R4GESTORM management reserves the right to decline, remove, or terminate any partnership at its discretion.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔄 Partnership Process

① Join R4GESTORM Community

② Contact the Owner, Advertising Strategist, or Brand Manager

③ Discuss and finalize the partnership agreement

④ Exchange advertisements

⑤ Both servers post each other's advertisements

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🤝 Final Note

Our goal is to build long-term partnerships with active, friendly, and genuine communities. If your server meets the requirements, we'd love to hear from you.
